# Roadmap

Where this product could go, in priority order. Shipped items move to the bottom.

## Next

- **Shareable report links.** A scanned site gets a stable address like `/r/example.com` that the owner can send to colleagues. Reports that travel bring their own visitors.
- **Score badge.** An embeddable "AI visibility A" badge for sites that score well, linking back to the scanner. Distribution that compounds.

## Later

- **Scan analytics.** A private count of scans per day and the domains scanned, to learn which industries are curious and tune outreach accordingly.
- **Rescan and compare.** Let a returning visitor scan again and see what improved since last time, which rewards people who fixed things and gives a reason to return.
- **Sector context in the report.** "Your score of 62 sits above the retail average of 54," using data already published in The Agentic Web Index.

## Ideas being weighed

- PDF export of the full report.
- A monitored mode, monthly automatic rescans with an email when the score changes.

## Shipped

- 2026-07-11, free scan with teaser grade, email unlocks the full plain English report on screen.
- 2026-07-11, leads stored as commits in a private GitHub repository, stateless server.
- 2026-07-11, hourly per visitor rate limit.
