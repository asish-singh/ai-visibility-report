Privacy

This page explains, in plain terms, what this site collects and why.

What we collect

When you scan a website, we record the address you entered and the scan results. If you request the full report, we also record the email address and name you provide.

How it is used

Your email is used only to follow up about your scan results and, if you are interested, about help fixing the issues found. It is never sold or shared with anyone else, and there is no automated mailing list.

What the scanner does to your site

The scan only reads public pages, the same way a search engine or an AI assistant would. It changes nothing on your site.

Questions or removal

If you want your details deleted, or have any question, contact Asish Singh and it will be handled promptly.
